"title": "Trezor: order data exposed in breach at delivery partner Skladon"
"description": "On 15 November, delivery partner Skladon told Trezor that an attacker had gained unauthorized access to Trezor order data it holds. Trezor believes data for about 27,000 orders placed between 9 August and 7 November was exported, including name, phone number, address, email and order details. Skladon delivers to many countries, so the incident is not limited to one region. Trezor states its own systems were not compromised and devices remain secure. The investigation with Skladon is ongoing."
