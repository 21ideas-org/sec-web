title: "Blink: an admin-tools flaw led to 35 account takeovers, 6.61 BTC withdrawn and contact data viewed for 3817 accounts"
description: "On September 19, an attacker exploited authorization flaws in Blink's administrative tools: anyone with a free account could grant themselves support privileges, change customers' email addresses and phone numbers, and log in to their accounts. About 6.61 BTC was withdrawn from 24 accounts through the operational wallet; Blink's shareholders reimbursed the customers, and cold multisig storage and non-self... non-custodial accounts were not affected. Data for another 3817 accounts was also viewed — in some cases a phone number or email; according to the company, names, documents, passwords and seeds were not disclosed. All 24 drained accounts lacked two-factor authentication, while the attacker was unable to withdraw from nine accounts with 2FA."
