title: "Keystone confirms its official website was breached: customer data and the device authenticity verification key leaked"
description: "According to the vendor, monitoring detected unauthorized access to the official website on October 20, 2026: an attacker escalated privileges through a vulnerability in a third-party data monitoring system, obtained some customer data and the WebAuth key used to verify device authenticity. The breach affected 25,964 customer records from 2024–2026 containing names, contact details, shipping addresses and order details, as well as 12,873 incomplete checkouts containing names, contact details and shipping information; purchases through Amazon and other channels were not affected. The vendor says the vulnerability has been fixed and unsafe website functions disabled; according to its information, recovery phrases, private keys and payment data were not affected. The key leak makes it possible for a counterfeit device to pass the authenticity check — production has switched to a new key, 18,835 devices in warehouse stock with the old key will be destroyed, and shipments of website orders have been suspended."
