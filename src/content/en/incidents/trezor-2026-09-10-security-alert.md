title: "Trezor: phishing email led to an app requesting wallet backups; about 2,500 people opened the link"
description: "Trezor has disclosed details. Brevo, the vendor's mailing platform, was breached; the incident affected 120 Brevo accounts. The email was sent to about 347,000 addresses in the newsletter database. The email link offered an app download that asked users to enter their wallet backup. Trezor blocked the domain after 20 minutes, but around 2,500 people had opened the link by then. The vendor cannot confirm whether the database was downloaded, so it considers all addresses known to the attacker. They may be used for further phishing campaigns."
