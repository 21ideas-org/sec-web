description: "Specter reports a compromise of the Brevo mailing service, which is used by several Bitcoin projects, including two companies that make hardware wallets. Phishing emails are already being sent through it — meaning the attackers have customer email lists and can write on behalf of brands familiar to readers. The typical goal of such mailings is to obtain a seed phrase under the guise of “mandatory verification” or an “urgent update,” or to send users to a lookalike website. Specter itself says that its subscribers and its own mailing list are unaffected because it uses a different provider; the message does not name the specific companies affected."
