description: "About two weeks ago, several owners of Ledger hardware wallets in Asia discovered outgoing transactions and empty balances. Researchers found soldered implants in these devices that read the screen contents and transmitted the seed phrase out through a cellular modem. Current information indicates that a single Ledger reseller in Asia made and installed the implants, then sold the devices to customers and other stores. Neither an authenticity check nor correct firmware detects this modification: the genuine device continues to operate normally while the added electronics remain unnoticed."
