title: "Ledger confirms a hardware implant in devices from reseller CryptoBilis: seed read from the screen during setup, funds withdrawn"
description: "Since October 9, people who bought Ledger devices through authorized reseller CryptoBilis (Malaysia, Indonesia, the Philippines) have been reporting empty wallets; Ledger confirmed that the affected device contained an unauthorized hardware implant. The implant does not touch the secure chip or change the transactions being signed — it reads what the screen displays, decodes the 24 words during initial setup and sends them out over a mobile network using an eSIM, so the device passed the built-in authenticity check. Ledger says all confirmed losses are linked to this reseller, while its direct sales channel shows no signs of compromise; the total has not been officially confirmed, and blockchain researchers estimate losses of $86–93 million across more than 300 wallets. Separately: on October 13, a major European retail chain suspended Ledger sales after a customer in Germany reported an implant, while an inspection of warehouse and retail stock in Germany found no signs of tampering."
